WHY USE THIS CALCULATOR?
A better estimate than simply counting bedrooms.

Two three-bedroom houses can be completely different window cleaning jobs.

One might have eight straightforward windows.

Another might have bay windows, French doors, Velux windows, a conservatory and awkward access.

That's why Window Cleaning Calculator considers the actual job, rather than assigning one generic price to every property of the same size.

HOW IT WORKS

What does window cleaning cost?

There isn't one standard UK window cleaning price.

A regular maintenance clean may cost considerably less than a one-off or first clean, while larger panes, difficult access, internal windows and conservatories can increase the amount of work involved.

Local pricing without pretending every street is the same.

Window cleaning prices vary across the country.

Our calculator uses the property and glazing first, then considers local market conditions and some of the extra costs involved in working in different areas.

A property in central London shouldn't automatically receive the same estimate as the equivalent property in a lower-cost market.

Likewise, an expensive rural or tourist area shouldn't be treated as cheap simply because it isn't in London
