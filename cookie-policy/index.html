WindowCleaningCalculator.co.uk aims to keep its use of cookies and similar technologies to a minimum.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites can store on your device to remember information or help website features work.

Cookies we currently use

At launch, WindowCleaningCalculator.co.uk does not intentionally use advertising or behavioural tracking cookies.

The calculator may use temporary browser storage to remember your answers while you move between calculator steps. This helps prevent your calculation from being lost if you move backwards or refresh the page.

This information is used to operate the calculator and is not used for advertising.

Third-party services

The website may communicate with services including Postcodes.io, Google and Brevo where necessary to provide calculator functionality, store submitted information or send requested emails.

Future analytics and advertising

We may introduce analytics or advertising services, including Google services, in the future.

If we introduce non-essential cookies or tracking technologies, we will update this policy and provide appropriate cookie controls before those technologies are used.

Managing cookies

You can manage or delete cookies and stored website data through your browser settings.
